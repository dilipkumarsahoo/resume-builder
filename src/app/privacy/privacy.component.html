Our 3 Core Privacy Commitments
  • 1. Zero Data Selling: We will never sell, rent, or trade your resumes or contact info to recruiters or advertisers.
  • 2. You Own Your Content: You retain complete ownership and deletion rights over your resumes.
  • 3. Bank-Grade Encryption: All stored documents and transmissions are encrypted using industry-standard protocols.
1

Introduction & Core Promise

At GlowCV, we believe your career journey and personal resume data are strictly private. This Privacy Policy describes how GlowCV ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and handles your information when you visit our website, utilize our resume builder, or interact with our services.

2

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to deliver resume creation and career management services:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, email address, encrypted authentication tokens.
  • Resume & Career Data: Employment history, education, skills, contact numbers, summary statements, projects, and portfolio links entered into our editor.
  • Payment Information: When upgrading to Pro, payments are processed directly by PhonePe Gateway. GlowCV does not store raw credit card numbers or UPI PINs.
  • Usage Analytics & Device Data: Browser type, IP address, page interactions, and diagnostic metrics to optimize site performance.
3

How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely for the following purposes:

  • Rendering, formatting, and generating PDF resumes and matching cover letters.
  • Powering AI-assisted bullet point suggestions, grammar improvements, and ATS keyword matching.
  • Processing subscription upgrades and managing Pro account benefits.
  • Providing responsive customer support and resolving technical queries.
  • Detecting and preventing fraud, unauthorized access, and security threats.
4

We Never Sell Your Data

GlowCV does NOT sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your resume data, contact information, or job application histories to third-party data brokers, marketers, or advertisers. Your career data belongs solely to you.

5

Third-Party Service Providers

To provide reliable service, we integrate with trusted third-party cloud infrastructure partners under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • PhonePe Payment Gateway: For PCI-DSS compliant secure subscription processing.
  • Cloud Hosting & Database: High-availability servers with automated backups and encrypted databases.
  • AI Infrastructure: Enterprise-grade AI APIs that do not use your personal resume data for general model training.
6

Data Security & Encryption

We implement robust organizational and technical safeguards:

  • 256-bit SSL / TLS encryption for all data in transit across browser and API endpoints.
  • Encrypted databases at rest to prevent unauthorized data access.
  • Continuous security monitoring and automated vulnerability audits.
7

Your Rights & Data Portability

You maintain full control over your data:

  • Export & Download: Export your resumes in PDF or plain format anytime without restrictions.
  • Edit & Update: Modify your personal and professional profile details directly in the editor.
  • Delete Account & Data: Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated documents by contacting support.
8
