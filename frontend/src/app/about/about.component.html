Our Mission

Why We Created GlowCV

Writing a resume shouldn't be stressful or time-consuming. Yet most professionals still wrestle with misaligned margins in Word or Google Docs, spending hours fighting formatting rather than telling their story.

Meanwhile, modern design tools and AI have revolutionized everyday productivity — so why is resume building still stuck in the past?

That's why we created GlowCV: a fast, intuitive, and genuinely delightful resume and cover letter builder. You focus on your achievements — we ensure flawless typography, modern layouts, and automated ATS compliance.
